Love is composed of a single soul inhabiting two bodies.

爱情是两个身体里居住着同一个灵魂.

### 单词

###### eradication
- 翻译: 根除
- 例句: They hoped that the eradication of weeds across the field would yield a better crop.

###### instructor
- 翻译: 指导者, 教员, 教练
- 例句: The driving instructor is helping his student learn how to back a car.
