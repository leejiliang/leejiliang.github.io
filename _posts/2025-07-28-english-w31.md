It is not in the stars to hold our destiny but in ourselves.

### word

#### incorporate
- 翻译: v 包含，使并入
- 例句: The app update incorporate many new features and improvements.

#### mild
- 翻译：n. 淡味啤酒， adj. 清淡的；温暖的；轻微的；温和的；和善的；
- 例句：I would love a bowl of mild chicken soup right now.

#### peg
- 翻译：n. 小钉， 栓，挂物钉；挂钩；晾衣夹；v. 用钉子钉；用钉子固定；限定
- Pegs can be used to hang tiny things on the wall.

#### sue
- v 控告，提起诉讼； 控告，起诉，请愿
- n 女子名（Sue)
- The victim and her lawyer are suing the company for the work-related injury.

#### eliminate
- v 消除
- vt 消除， 根除，淘汰
- The government of a country with a balance of payments deficit will usually be expected to take measures to reduce or eliminate the dificit.

#### perpetual
- adj. 永远的，永恒的，不断的
- There is perpetual snowfall on this mountain as it snows all year round.

#### vacancy
- n. 空， 空白，空房； 空缺；空虚
- There is a job vacancy-let me apply!

#### thermal
- adj. 保暖的；温泉的；热的
- n 保暖内衣裤
- Gloves, scarfs and thermal underwear help keep you warm in winter.
